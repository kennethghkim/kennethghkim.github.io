## About

I'm Kenneth, a Business Administration and Data Science student at Northeastern University's D'Amore-McKim School of Business, concentrating in Finance. I'm at my best making things run better, whether it's an operations process, a project, or a tool. Finance is where I most want to do it, because money flows to its best use and good numbers show where that is.

I find the structure fast. At my internship, every client had its own billing spreadsheet, but it all came down to a few contract terms. I built the system on those and left the one-off cases to people.

I make work hold up. Recurring problems become tools or rules, and I leave things easy to hand off. The [billing app](/projects/billing-app/) went to two colleagues who weren't developers, with step-by-step training.

I know where my knowledge ends. Anything unclear or outside my call, I flag early, like a late payment I raised before it became a problem. Where my own work has weak spots, I add structure: [simpleharness](/projects/simpleharness/) began as guardrails for my work with AI coding agents.
