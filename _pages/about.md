## About

I'm Kenneth, a Business Administration and Data Science student at Northeastern University's D'Amore-McKim School of Business, concentrating in Finance. My interests are in financial analysis, investing, and data-driven automation, especially work that turns financial data into clear, reliable decisions.

My recent work includes an [in-house billing application](/projects/billing-app/) built in Python and SQLite that cut statement preparation time by 67%, and a [Python pipeline](/projects/trade-analysis/) that analyzes about 1,200 of my own trades to refine my investment rules. I like problems where the result can be measured and checked against the numbers. I also bring operations experience from a Coupang fulfillment center and leadership experience as a squad leader in the Republic of Korea Army and as the founder and president of my high school investing club.
