## Key Findings

| Area | Oracle | SAP | Reading |
|---|---|---|---|
| Profitability | ROE 82.4%, ROA 8.04% | ROE 7.08%, ROA 4.42% | High ROE comes partly from leverage |
| Liquidity | Current 0.75, quick 0.61 | Current 1.12, quick 0.94 | Weak on paper, driven by the CapEx surge |
| Solvency | D/E 7.03, TIE 4.96 | D/E 0.62, TIE 11.16 | Heavy debt, but interest is covered |
| Market | EPS $4.46, P/E 37.11 | EPS $2.80, P/E 88.88 | Priced far below its peer |

- **Liquidity strain:** CapEx +209% ($6.9B → $21.2B) vs. operating cash flow +11.5%, funded by new debt
- **Credit risk:** CapEx/Sales 13% → 37%, CDS near 80 bp, net debt/EBITDA 383%
- **Earnings quality:** server life 5 → 6 years added $573M, judged a reasonable estimate
