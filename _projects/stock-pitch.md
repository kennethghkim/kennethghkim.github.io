## Valuation

| DCF step | Value |
|---|--:|
| PV of free cash flow, 2026–2033 | $633M |
| PV of terminal value (1% growth) | $807M |
| Enterprise value | $1,440M |
| Net debt (50% of credit lines counted as debt) | $876M |
| Equity value | $564M |
| **Price target per share** | **$50.94** |

The model counts half of FOA's $1,076M in credit lines as debt, since part of it is bridge financing before securitization.
